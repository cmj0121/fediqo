Fediqo is a client for the open social web and nothing else. There is no Fediqo account, no
Fediqo server, and nothing to sign up for here: it reads the servers you point it at, from the
device it is installed on.

So there is no demo account to hand you, and none is needed to see the app work:

1. Launch it. The first screen asks which protocol to read. Mastodon is the one this build
   speaks; the others are listed and say they are not ready rather than being hidden.
2. Pick a server from the suggested list -- mastodon.social is in it -- or type any other
   Mastodon host.
3. The timeline fills. Public and Trending are read with no account at all.

Signing in is optional, and adds only what an account can do: the home timeline, and posting.
It is the server's own OAuth page, opened in a browser; Fediqo never sees a password, and the
token it is given is kept in the Keychain on the device.

Nothing is collected and nothing is sent anywhere except to the servers you named, which is
why the privacy answer is Data Not Collected. The source is public and the claim is checkable:
https://github.com/cmj0121/fediqo
