Every way there is to read.

0.1.0 asked that you could read a timeline, say something, and answer somebody. This one asks that every way there is to read is the same way — one shape, one set of keys, one place a post is written down, whatever question was asked to get it.

• Timelines made of hashtags, asked for by tag rather than sieved out of a timeline asked for something else. A tag on a post can be pressed, and typing # finds it
• One server's own writers, or everything but them
• Search what this device already holds, with the network off and without asking anybody. Your own servers are asked only after you say they may
• Inbox and Conversations, as a page of their own. Six people favouriting one post is one row that says six
• Your own page, the posts somebody asked you to read first, and the ones you have not sent yet
• Ask an opened post what it says now, and what it knows about itself: when exactly, and who favourited or boosted it
• Make a timeline out of several rules — this hashtag but not from that server — and see what they let through before you save it

Nothing is collected. There is no Fediqo server for anything to be collected on.
