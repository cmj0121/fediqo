The first release.

Fediqo reads several servers as one timeline. Add a Mastodon-family server or a
Discuz! forum by typing its address; the app looks at it and shows you what it
says about itself -- what it runs, whether it can be read without an account,
how many people are on it -- before you decide to take it. Nothing is added
until you say so.

Don't know an address? Browse by protocol and pick from the servers suggested
for it. The browser reaches nobody until you choose a protocol, so opening it
to see what this app reads tells no third party anything about you.

Your servers live on one page, a row each: what it is, what it said about
itself, and what you can do to it -- sign in or out where that means anything,
change which boards you read, clear what it left on this device, or let it go.
Letting go takes its posts and its cached pictures with it.

Forums are read as forums. A discussion has a title, a board it was posted in
and a count of how many have answered. Pick the boards you want, one by one,
and each becomes its own place in the rail.
