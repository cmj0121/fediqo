One timeline. Every network.

Fediqo reads the servers you choose and gives you one stream, in one order. A post that reached you from two
of them is one row rather than two, and the row says which servers carried it.

Nothing is ranked, scored or re-ordered on the way. The only thing between what arrived and what you see is a
rule you wrote.

THERE IS NO FEDIQO SERVER

Your iPhone talks to your servers and to nobody else. There is no account with us, no analytics, no crash
reporting and no third-party SDK, so there is nowhere for a profile of you to be kept. Your access tokens are
in the Keychain and your timeline is in one database on the device.

WHAT IT DOES

• Several servers, merged into one timeline, in one order
• Every row says which servers handed the post over
• Write once and send it out, each server you chose told exactly once
• Threads, profiles, images and video, in a native interface
• A statistics screen that says what is kept here, and what was asked of other people's servers
• English and 繁體中文

Today Fediqo speaks Mastodon. It is built so that the next protocol is another source in the same timeline
rather than another tab.
